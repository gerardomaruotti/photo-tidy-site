Good to know

Questions,
answered.

Does PhotoTidy upload my photos?

No. Indexing, visual analysis, quality scoring, grouping, and explanations all run on your device. PhotoTidy does not upload your photos or metadata for review.

Will it delete photos automatically?

Never. Deletion is limited to the Compare screen, where you choose what to keep, review every suggestion, and confirm with the system photo-deletion prompt.

What if some of my photos are in iCloud?

PhotoTidy keeps iCloud-backed scanning off by default. You can opt in from Settings when you want the system photo library to fetch originals for analysis.

Can I try it without granting photo access?

Yes. Demo mode is a first-class experience, so you can explore Review, Library, and Compare before sharing access to your library.
